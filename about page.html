About The Bridge

The Bridge is a website concept that connects organizations that have openings with people looking for opportunities to fill them. It is a semester project for CS 4345 at Faulkner University. It is a classroom concept, not an official Faulkner University service, and any organizations or listings you see while we build it are examples, not real partners.

Why The Bridge exists

Local churches regularly need help, from staffing children's ministry and organizing outreach events to filling part-time roles or offering internship-style experience in ministry, communications, and administration. Today those needs are scattered across bulletin announcements, personal connections, and word of mouth, which makes them hard for students to discover and hard for churches to share with a wide, willing audience.

At the same time, students often need service hours, internship placements, or hands-on course experience, but there is no single place that connects them to nearby churches that could offer it. The Bridge is our attempt to be that place.

Who it serves

The Bridge is designed first for churches near Faulkner University and for the Faulkner students, faculty, and departments who are looking for volunteer, internship, or paid opportunities with them. Students may apply on their own, to meet a course requirement, or for a formal internship.

As we build the site, visitors will be able to:

  • Post an opportunity, with its type, time commitment, location, and description (organizations).
  • Browse and filter open opportunities and apply to the ones that fit (students).
  • Find which opportunities count toward a course requirement (faculty and departments).

How we are building it

We are a small team of students building The Bridge in stages: pages first, then forms, then a server and database. A few commitments guide our choices.

  • Honest communication. This page says what is real and what is still planned.
  • Collect only what we need. Our forms will not ask for government ID numbers, payment details, or home addresses.
  • Accessible by default. We are using labeled form fields, keyboard-friendly navigation, and readable color contrast.
  • Room to grow. We write the site generally enough that it could someday serve other schools and organizations.
