The Bridge

Project Overview:
The Bridge connects organizations that have openings with people looking for opportunities to fill them. In its first version, that means local churches near Faulkner University posting volunteer roles, internships, and part-time positions, and Faulkner students, faculty, and departments browsing and applying to those roles personally, as a course requirement, or as a formal internship. The main task a visitor should be able to complete is: a partner organization can post an opportunity, and a student can find that opportunity and submit an application to it. 
The intended audience for The Bridge is for the Faulkner University community (students and staff) and surrounding churches. So, the students, faculty, and departments looking for volunteer, internship, or paid opportunities with said churches.

Pages and Features:
- Home Page: Introduces The Bridge in one sentence and links visitors to Browse Opportunities or Post an Opportunity.
- Browse Opportunities: A filterable table of all open opportunities (organization, title, type, time commitment, deadline). The main entry point for students.
- Opportunity Detail: The full description of one opportunity, with an Apply button that leads to the Apply form.
- Post an Opportunity: A form where a partner organization submits a new listing (see the separate wireframe).
- Apply / My Applications: A form for a student to apply to a specific opportunity, plus a simple view of what they have already submitted.
